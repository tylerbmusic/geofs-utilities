# Installation process:  
Step 1: Copy the contents of 'userscript.js' into your clipboard  
Step 2: If you haven't already, download Tampermonkey (https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/tampermonkey/dhdgffkkebhmkfjojejmpbldmpobfkfo//Open)  
Step 3: Press the Extensions icon on the toolbar at the top of your browser and click on the icon that looks like a rounded square with two eyes at the bottom, and press "Dashboard"  
Step 4: Press the little "+" tab, delete everything in the big text box, paste your clipboard contents into that text box, and press Ctrl/Cmd + S to save it.  
Step 5: Open GeoFS, and it should work! 

Note: When installing Tampermonkey, you may need to enable Developer Mode inside of the browser's settings, and click "Allow User Scripts" in Tampermonkey's browser settings.

# About

GeoFS Utilities has a bunch of features that were too small to be their own addon.  
It adds various suggestions by bili-開飛機のzm, VR PoZz, bluga4893, GTAIV, and suggestions by discord users (idk who):
- 10 spoiler positions,
- auto brakes/spoilers/reverse thrust,
- a light that you could pretend is a landing light,
- a key to make the elevator trim match the aileron pitch,
- highly customizable aerobatic smoke,
- a G-Force Meter,
- an AoA meter,
- an option to reset cam when the mouse button is released, and
- an option that allows you to rotate the camera without clicking the mouse button.

## How to use
After installing the addon, go to [GeoFS](https://www.geo-fs.com/geofs.php), wait for the page to load, and click on the "ADDON SETTINGS" button at the bottom. From there, you can enable, disable, and customize every feature.
